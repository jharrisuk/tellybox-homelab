Who can do what?

Viewer: watch and request films and shows. Downloads need to be enabled for their account.

Admin: also approve devices, manage settings and see activity. Only give this to someone you trust to manage Tellybox.

Pending requests expire after 24 hours. Approved devices have access for a year. Revoking access stops online access; existing offline downloads remain on the device.

Browser access & remote password

Guests sign in away from home. Approved apps keep their own device access; local web access needs no login.
