Who can do what?

Viewer: watch and request films and shows. Downloads need to be enabled for their account.

Admin: also approve devices, manage settings and see activity. Only give this to someone you trust to manage Tellybox.

Link codes: a phone or TV can show an 8-character code. Open the account, use Link device and enter the code. Each account has a phone and TV allowance, adjustable under Allowances. Manual approval and linking still work as before.

Pending requests expire after 24 hours. Approved devices have access for a year. Revoking access stops online access; existing offline downloads remain on the device.

Browser access & remote password

Guests sign in away from home. Approved apps keep their own device access; local web access needs no login.

    LOCAL FILES

    Export to USB

    Copy original local films, episodes and completed recordings to a USB drive plugged into the media host.

    Checking USB export...

    Originals are never deleted or changed. Existing files on the USB drive are never overwritten. Sidecar subtitles are copied when available. Unmount the drive from the media host before unplugging it.
