WHY ANOTHER MEDIA APP?

Because apparently I needed another homelab project.

I’ve been a developer for over 20 years. My home media servers worked brilliantly, but watching something meant too many apps, menus and faffing about. I wanted one place the whole family could use without a demonstration.

So I built Tellybox: my collection, live TV, 24/7 channels and on-demand content, together. With Codex by my side, a little local player became an ungodly number of evenings tinkering. It’s even wife-approved now.

Friends asked for their own version, so I turned the bits specific to my house into settings. Same code, your server, your services. It’s a geek’s hobby project I’m proud to share. Hopefully it saves you some faff too.

- James · developer, homelabber, serial tinkerer
